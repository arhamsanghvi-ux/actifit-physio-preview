What the clinic treats

Services

Areas of care listed on the clinic’s public profiles. Call or WhatsApp to check if your concern is covered.

01

Orthopaedic physiotherapy

Back pain, neck pain, shoulder pain, knee pain, heel pain (plantar fasciitis) and joint pain.

02

Spine care

Back pain physiotherapy, thoracic spine concerns, PIVD (disc-related) care and posture correction.

03

Sports injury rehab

Rehabilitation for sports injuries and a guided return to activity.

04

Post-operative rehabilitation

Post-operative fracture care and rehabilitation after surgery, including joint replacement.

05

Neuro rehabilitation

Neurofunctional and neuromuscular physiotherapy.

06

Women’s health

Pregnancy and post-delivery physiotherapy, plus pre- and post-natal fitness.

07

Arthritis care

Arthritis physiotherapy, osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis management.

08

Geriatric physiotherapy

Physiotherapy for older adults, plus electrotherapy and industrial physiotherapy.

About

A neighbourhood physio clinic in Borivali West

ActiFit Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation Clinic is on the ground floor of Malti Apartment, RM Bhattad Road, near Shimpoli, opposite Yogesh Tower, in Borivali West.

The clinic offers orthopaedic, sports, neuro and women’s health physiotherapy, along with posture correction and rehabilitation after surgery.

Not sure what you need? Call or send a WhatsApp message describing your concern, and the clinic can tell you how to go ahead.

  • Orthopaedics
  • Sports rehab
  • Pre & post natal fitness
  • Posture correction
  • Neuro rehab
